## Approved by Cosmin

1. Model: a roof = footprint polygon + a record per footprint edge {pitch (90 = vertical: gable end or edge against a wall), eave height, later profile = list of (height, pitch) breaks}. The roof record stores defaults; edges store overrides only. Geometry from a weighted straight skeleton.
2. Per-edge overrides are anchored by a stored point near the edge, like room points (reuse room point storage/undo/per-fix map/editor move+remove; own hit test: nearest footprint edge).
3. Eave height is relative (to the wall top of the floor below) and can be negative.
4. Own skeleton implementation for axis-aligned plans (floors are `orthogonal`), not CGAL.
5. Tie rule: where two parallel edges facing the same way line up, the shallower (faster) edge continues over both (lower roof).
6. Regions closed in by vertical edges get no roof face; the editor flags them later.
7. Top-floor ceiling: keep the flat top slab; default eave height = `house.slab_thickness`; `roof.eave_h` null = that default, resolved at use.
8. Default area kind with no area point: ROOF. Different-pitch join: step on the underside.

## Combination rules (Cosmin: "got it")

- R1: overlapping roofs: take the highest surface (dormers, low roof against a higher roof, valleys). Outward-bent breaks (gambrel) come from a profile break on one roof.
- R2: the code cuts walls and slabs of all floors below by the roof underside, and raises or lowers exterior walls on a footprint edge to the roof underside (gable triangles, knee walls, A-frame, ridge lowered into the floor below).
- R3: planes are defined at the wall line; the overhang contour only clips the extended planes. Default contour = wall line offset by overhang; custom = drawn polygon.
- Default footprint = a floor's underflow region; per region the user picks own roof / terrace / covered by the upper roof's extended footprint (ties into the deferred slab-ownership design in js/HOUSE-WALLS.txt). Dormers = attic-floor walls + a second roof with a drawn footprint.
- Editor handles map to stored values by direct formulas (<= 2 unknowns); equal ridge heights etc. are drag snaps, not stored constraints.

## Geometry design: union of prisms (settled, Cosmin: "got it, ok")

Cosmin rejected roof-owned gable faces (the wall must own the gable) and wanted a design that later stages don't rewrite.

- Prism = plan region between a bottom plane and a top plane (z = a*x + b*y + c). Every house part is prisms: walls, slabs, roof bodies (per roof face: face polygon extended to the overhang contour, underside -> underside + t/cos(pitch)).
- R2 as prisms: cut (top = min(top, underside), split on the crossing line) and raise (exterior wall bodies of the roof's floor, floor top -> underside): gable, knee wall, lowered eave are one operation. R3: eave faces extended outward, bounded by extended hip lines at outside corners; at inside corners overlap + union gives the valley.
- No 3D subtraction: holes are built in 2D (stairwell = slab minus opening; window = wall split into below-sill and above-head prisms). Sides always vertical. Later per-material faces need the merge keyed by material.
- Dormers: walls up to the dormer roof underside; inside needs an opening in the main roof over the dormer interior and trimming the dormer roof where its underside drops below the main underside. Unverified sliver between the two lines; check on a prototype.
- Different pitches meeting: step on the underside (each face body = prism over its own top-face polygon). Off-plumb join later = underside from a second skeleton run with lowered eaves.
- Data (proposed, part of the design, not built): floor `roofs: [{footprint (null = overflow areas tagged roof; drawn = dormer/extra), pitch, eave_h, overhang, edge_points}]` (replaces `floor.roof`); floor `area_points: [{x, y, kind}]` per overflow area, kind = own roof / terrace / covered by upper roof.
